Template Guide

Email templates allow CampusPay to reuse professionally formatted messages throughout the system.

  • Give every template a unique slug.
  • Keep the subject short and meaningful.
  • Provide plain-text fallback content.
  • Use HTML for professional formatting.
  • Deactivate templates that should not be sent.
